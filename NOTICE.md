## Inspiration
The Paths tab (structured, topic-based courses with progress tracking and notes) is inspired by
**HackerLearn** by Preet Shukla (https://github.com/PreetSHUKLA/hackerlearn, MIT License), which built
roadmaps from the HackerTube catalog. No HackerLearn code or course lists were copied: the paths in this
app are generated by tools/preprocess.py from the catalog's topics and difficulty levels.

## Optional on-device AI
If enabled by the user, the app downloads the LaMini-Flan-T5-77M model (Xenova/LaMini-Flan-T5-77M on
Hugging Face) and runs transformers.js (https://github.com/xenova/transformers.js, Apache-2.0) locally.
